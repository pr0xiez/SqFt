TOOL 01 — ESTIMATINGCORE

Sketch the room. Get the area.

Tap to add a room, type the dimensions, and SqFt handles the math — coverage, waste factor, box counts, the whole brief.

  • 01.1

    Room sketcher

    Multiple rooms per estimate. Square footage updates live as you type.

  • 01.2

    Line items with units

    Hardwood, LVP, tile, carpet, labor, demo, padding — each with its own unit, coverage, and waste default.

  • 01.3

    Auto-saving builder

    Edit at the kitchen table or in the truck. Your work is saved automatically — close the laptop, pick it up on your phone.

  • 01.4

    Branded PDF export

    Logo, license number, totals, deposit — issued as a clean PDF the homeowner respects.

TOOL 02 — APPROVALCORE

One link. Zero login.

Send a token-signed URL. The customer reviews the breakdown and e-signs from their phone, tablet, or laptop — no app, no account.

  • 02.1

    Public customer portal

    Token-scoped, read-only quote view. No PII leak, no orphan invites.

  • 02.2

    Locked at signature

    The signed quote is sealed — nobody can quietly edit the number after the customer approves it.

  • 02.3

    Email + SMS delivery

    Send the link the way the customer actually checks. Status updates on the estimate as it's opened & signed.

  • 02.4

    Status that actually means something

    Draft → Sent → Viewed → Signed → Scheduled. Visible on the pipeline kanban.

TOOL 03 — SCHEDULECORE

Right crew. Right job. Right day.

Drag an install onto the calendar, assign the crew, and SqFt flags conflicts before they bite. Field crews see only what they need to see.

  • 03.1

    Week + month view

    Click a slot to create a job. Drag to move. Conflict warnings on overlapping crew assignments.

  • 03.2

    Multi-crew assignment

    Schedule the install, the measure, the punch-list. Per-crew filter on the schedule view.

  • 03.3

    Customer-linked events

    Every event ties back to the customer + estimate. One click to the quote, one click to the address.

  • 03.4

    Crew-only view CREW PLAN

    Installers see their assigned jobs and addresses. They don't see margins, pipeline, or pricing.

TOOL 04 — PIPELINE & CUSTOMERSCORE

Where every job actually stands.

A real dashboard, not a vanity report. Drag estimates through the pipeline, see today's schedule, watch the 6-month revenue chart move.

  • 04.1

    Pipeline kanban

    Draft → Sent → Scheduled → Invoiced → Won/Lost. Drag-and-drop status changes that log to the audit trail.

  • 04.2

    KPI dashboard

    Open opportunities, sent quotes awaiting reply, deposits collected, this-month revenue. The numbers that matter at 6am.

  • 04.3

    Customer record

    Every estimate, event, and note in one place. Phone, email, address, source — searchable across the whole org.

  • 04.4

    Six-month revenue chart

    Hover any month to see the jobs behind the number. Prints clean for the bank meeting.

TOOL 05 — MONEY & BOOKSCREW

Get paid before you're off-site.

Cards and ACH through Stripe Connect, two-way QuickBooks Online sync, real-time job costing that catches margin leaks before you eat them.

  • 05.1

    Stripe Connect payments

    Card + ACH on the same customer link. Funds settle direct to your bank in T+2.

  • 05.2

    QuickBooks Online sync

    Connect once from settings. Invoices push to QuickBooks the moment they're created; payments mark themselves paid back in SqFt.

  • 05.3

    Job costing in the quote

    Materials, labor hours, overhead costed as you build. Margin $ and % update line-by-line.

  • 05.4

    Deposit collection

    Configure a deposit % on the org. Collect at signature, see remaining balance through install.
