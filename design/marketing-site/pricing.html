03 / Field Questions

Things people actually ask.

Q.01

Do I need a card to start the trial?

Yes. We ask for a card up front so you don't lose a day of measurement work at the end of the trial. You won't be charged until day 15, and you can cancel anytime from settings before then.

Q.02

What happens on day 15?

The card on file is charged for the plan you selected at signup. Everything you built during the trial — estimates, customers, schedule — stays exactly where you left it.

Q.03

Can I switch plans later?

Anytime, in both directions. Upgrade and you get the new features immediately, prorated. Downgrade and the change takes effect at the next billing cycle.

Q.04

What about 9+ crew seats?

Talk to us. Larger shops get a Crew+ tier with volume seat pricing, custom roles, SSO, and a dedicated success contact. Email sales@sqft.dev.

Q.05

Who owns the data?

You do. Cancel and we export everything to CSV — customers, estimates, line items, schedule history. No lock-in, no last-mile gotcha.

Q.06

How are payments processed?

Stripe Connect on the Crew plan. Funds settle directly to your bank in T+2, fees come out of the transaction, and refunds work how you'd expect. We never touch the money.
